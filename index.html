01 / Profile

About

My work sits at the intersection of discourse, interaction, cognition, and computation.

Dr. Shuangshuang Chen is a Lecturer at the Institute of Modern Languages and Linguistics, Fudan University, China. She received her Ph.D. in Linguistics from the University of Auckland, New Zealand.

Her research lies at the intersection of pragmatics, interactional linguistics, computational linguistics, and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on reference and anaphora, pragmatic inference, discourse understanding, and the evaluation of large language models and intelligent agents. She investigates how humans and AI systems understand language, interpret context, manage ambiguity, and generate context-sensitive responses.

A central theme of her work is how linguistic forms are selected and interpreted in discourse and interaction, and how these choices contribute to coherence, salience management, and intersubjective understanding. Her Routledge monograph provides a corpus-based and computational account of null subjects in Mandarin Chinese. Her recent work extends these insights to pragmatic reasoning, instruction following, commonsense reasoning, reference resolution, and interpretable AI evaluation.

Dr. Chen has published more than ten research articles and a scholarly monograph. She has led national and provincial-level projects on Chinese discourse, anaphora processing, and computational modelling, and has been selected for the Shanghai Pujiang Talent Program and the Shanghai Young Leading Talent Program.

陈双双，复旦大学现代语言学研究院青年副研究员，获新西兰奥克兰大学语言学博士学位。

她的研究位于语用学、互动语言学、计算语言学与人工智能的交叉领域，重点关注指称与回指、语用推理、话语理解，以及大语言模型和智能体的评测。她致力于探究人类与人工智能系统如何理解语言、解释语境、处理歧义，并在复杂交际环境中生成符合语境的回应。

她的研究核心之一是阐释语言形式在话语与互动中如何被选择和解读，以及这些选择如何促进话语连贯、显著性管理与主体间理解。其 Routledge 学术专著以语料库和计算方法系统研究现代汉语零形主语的类型、话语功能与消解机制。近期研究进一步将语言学理论应用于大语言模型的语用推理、指令遵循、常识推理、指称消解及可解释评测。

陈博士已在国内外期刊和会议发表十余篇研究论文，并出版学术专著一部。她主持多项国家级和省部级项目，入选上海市浦江人才计划和上海领军人才计划青年人才项目。

02 / Inquiry

Research

Six connected strands guide my research into how meaning is produced, recovered, and modeled by humans and AI systems.

01

Pragmatics

Pragmatic inference, pragmatic strategies in social media, and the syntax–pragmatics interface.

02

Interactional Linguistics

How grammatical choices organize turns, actions, and social relations in conversation.

03

Chinese Discourse

The distribution and functions of linguistic forms across Chinese discourse and genres.

04

Reference & Anaphora

Zero anaphora, reference tracking, and discourse-level resolution mechanisms.

05

Computational Linguistics

Computational modelling of discourse, anaphora processing, and context-sensitive language understanding.

06

AI & Language

Linguistically informed, interpretable evaluation of LLMs and intelligent agents across pragmatic inference, reference resolution, instruction following, commonsense reasoning, and context-dependent decisions.
